SRTM FSM

A JCOP finite state machine for the SRTM boards on the LAr signal processors: every board, its LASP and its IPMC reduced to one state, carried up the tree, with sends carried down.

University of Arizona ATLAS group, DCS prototype on WinCC OA with the CERN JCOP framework. A public summary: the code, tests and records are kept in a private repository. Updated 19 Oct 2026.

Working assumptions

Placeholders for facts we don't have yet. Each sits behind one boundary so the real thing can replace it.

LASP
No separate state machine. 10 placeholder signals in their own device unit under each SRTM board.
Write path
Exists but unspecified. All sends go through one srtm_send stub with an ack readback.
Register data
The real board (datapoint SRTM, the board's address). The replay ghost (SRTM_Ghost01, a local address) stays connected as a reserve and as the seed of the M5 fleet.
IPMC
Up to 10 error types as a 10-bit mask with a severity table. valid=false means ERROR.
Send rules
SEND from OK and WARNING, RECOVER from ERROR, nothing from SENDING (Deck 1, slide 15).
